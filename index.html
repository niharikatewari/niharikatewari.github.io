Active Research Programs

Track 1: Explainable AI for Dementia Diagnosis and Brain Connectivity

Research on explainable graph neural networks for understanding brain connectivity, identifying neurological biomarkers, and supporting dementia diagnosis. Our paper, “Explainable Graph Neural Networks: Understanding Brain Connectivity and Biomarkers in Dementia,” has been accepted for publication in ACM Transactions on Computing for Healthcare. The research focuses on interpretable graph learning approaches for investigating brain network alterations and their relevance to neurodegenerative diseases. Accepted.

Track 2: Brain Graph Learning for Psychiatric Diagnosis

Research on data-efficient brain graph learning and graph augmentation for psychiatric diagnosis. Our paper, “Structure Matters: Brain Graph Augmentation via Learnable Edge Masking for Data-efficient Psychiatric Diagnosis,” was accepted at the Australasian Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AJCAI 2025). The work investigates learnable edge masking to improve brain graph representations under limited-data conditions. Accepted — AJCAI 2025.

Track 3: Graph Transformers for Neurodevelopmental Disorder Classification

Research on graph transformer architectures and mixture-of-experts learning for neurodevelopmental disorder classification. Our manuscript, “Multiscale Mixture-of-Experts Graph Transformer for Neurodevelopmental Disorder Classification,” was submitted to IEEE Transactions on Cognitive and Developmental Systems in July 2026 and is currently under review. Under Review.
